These are excellent feature proposals. They show a very deep and practical understanding of the actual realities faced by field engineers working on mini-grid sites, particularly in areas with harsh sunlight and spotty internet.

Here is my breakdown of the list:

The Most Critical/Impactful Features:

7. PWA / Offline Mode & Sync: This is arguably the most important feature on the list. Mini-grid sites are often in remote areas with zero connectivity. If the app doesn't work offline, it simply can't be used when it's needed most.
5. Auto-Save & Background Draft Recovery: We just built that massive 10-page Quarterly Audit form. While we added a manual "Save as Draft" button, an automatic IndexedDB backup every 10 seconds is a lifesaver. There is nothing more frustrating than losing 30 minutes of form entry because a phone battery died or the browser refreshed.
1. Auto-Calculations (PR, Yield, Efficiency): Automating the math for Specific Yield, AC/DC efficiency, and fuel burn rates will drastically reduce human error. Allowing them to be editable is a smart safeguard in case the physical meters are known to be faulty and the engineer needs to apply a manual correction.
Great Compliance & UI Features:

4. GPS Geolocation: A classic and highly effective compliance tool to prevent "armchair reporting" and ensure the technician is actually physically on-site.
6. Outdoor High-Contrast Sunlight Mode: This is my favorite feature on the list because it's so thoughtful. High-contrast UI toggles specifically designed for reading screens under direct Nigerian sunlight shows a perfect understanding of the end user's daily environment.
2. Structured Revision (Diffs): This will make the admin's life much easier. When a report is flagged and returned, seeing exactly what the technician changed (v1 to v2) without having to read the whole report again is a massive time-saver.
3. PDF Generation: We actually built the foundation for this in the Quarterly Report, but expanding it system-wide as a one-click option for all approved documents is essential for official record-keeping.

This is a very robust product roadmap. Which of these features would you like to tackle first? Converting the app to an offline-capable PWA (Feature 7) or adding the Auto-Calculations (Feature 1) might be great places to start.
