Here are custom, high-impact feature suggestions specifically tailored to the real-world operational challenges of managing solar hybrid mini-grids in Nigeria (e.g., rural GVE sites like Bisanti, Kuka, Kolokalo).

These are grouped by operational impact:

1. Operational Intelligence & Cost Control (Anti-Theft & Asset Protection)
⚡ A. Diesel Fuel Pilferage & Genset Efficiency Anomaly Engine
The Reality on the Ground: Fuel theft and "phantom generator runs" are among the highest operational expense (OpEx) leaks for rural mini-grids.
How It Works:
Automatically cross-references logged Generator Run-Hours, Diesel Consumed (Liters), and Energy Generated (kWh) from the Weekly and Hourly logs.
Uses standard diesel generator specific fuel consumption curves (typically $0.28 \text{ to } 0.33\text{ L/kWh}$).
The Alert: If a technician logs 4 hours of generator run time and 60 Liters consumed, but only 35 kWh produced (burn rate $> 1.7\text{ L/kWh}$), the system flags an immediate amber/red badge:
⚠️ Fuel Discrepancy Alert: Specific fuel consumption (1.71 L/kWh) is 420% above nominal. Suspected fuel leakage, theft, or severe engine injector fault.

Value: Saves millions of Naira annually in fuel loss and prevents unrecorded diesel diversion.
🔋 B. Battery Bank Degradation & Deep Discharge (DoD) Early Warning
The Reality on the Ground: Battery storage (Lithium-ion or tubular gel/lead-acid) accounts for 40%–50% of the entire capital cost of a mini-grid. Running batteries too deep destroys their cycle life.
How It Works:
Analyzes hourly Battery Voltage, SoC, and SoH trends across days.
Automatically flags whenever a site's battery bank drops below safe depth-of-discharge thresholds (e.g., SoC $< 20%$ or voltage $< 46\text{V}$ on a 48V bank) during night-time operation.
Tracks SOH degradation rate week-over-week to predict battery replacement cycles before unexpected blackouts happen.
📉 C. T&D Loss & Revenue Leakage Calculator
The Reality on the Ground: Power theft (illegal bypasses, meter tampering) and faulty distribution lines cause unaccounted energy loss.
How It Works:
Compares Total Energy Sent Out from the powerhouse busbar (kWh) vs. Total Customer Vending / Energy Billed (kWh) from the weekly reports.
Auto-computes Transmission & Distribution (T&D) Loss %: $$\text{T&D Loss (%)} = \left(\frac{\text{Energy Sent Out} - \text{Energy Billed}}{\text{Energy Sent Out}}\right) \times 100%$$
Automatically warns when technical/commercial losses exceed 10%–12%, suggesting line inspection or anti-tamper meter sweeps in the village.
2. Field Productivity & Technician UX (Built for Nigerian Rural Conditions)
📸 D. Automated EXIF Extraction & Tamper-Proof Canvas Watermarking
The Reality on the Ground: Technicians sometimes upload old photos from their phone gallery or recycled images from previous weeks instead of taking live photos of site equipment.
How It Works:
When a technician snaps or uploads a photo in 

ReportPhotoUploader.tsx
, ReportFlow draws it onto an HTML5 canvas and permanently burns a high-contrast forensic watermark in the bottom corner:
[GVE MINI-GRID — BISANTI]
[LAT: 9.1245° N | LON: 5.6812° E] [ACCURACY: ±6m]
[TIMESTAMP: 17-SEP-2026 11:42 AM WAT]
[INSPECTOR: S. IBRAHIM | ID: ENG-04]
Watermarked images cannot be spoofed and are legally and regulatorily admissible for insurance claims, warranty replacements, and donor audits.
📶 E. Low-Bandwidth "Tiered Sync" Mode (Text First, Media Later)
The Reality on the Ground: Many rural sites only have 2G/EDGE or intermittent network signals that drop when uploading 5MB of camera images.
How It Works:
Tier 1 (Instant): The text and numerical report data (< 15 KB) syncs immediately over low-bandwidth 2G connection.
Tier 2 (Background / Deferred): High-resolution photos stay queued in IndexedDB and automatically upload later when the phone detects a stable 3G/4G or Wi-Fi connection (or compresses them down to $< 100\text{ KB}$ webp).
Emergency SMS/WhatsApp String Generator: If there is zero data connectivity, a one-tap button generates a short 140-character encrypted text summary string the technician can SMS or WhatsApp to the control room.
🎙️ F. Voice-to-Text Maintenance Notes (Hands-Free Dictation)
The Reality on the Ground: Field engineers wearing grease-covered safety gloves while working inside generator sheds or distribution kiosks hate typing long paragraphs on tiny phone keypads.
How It Works:
A microphone icon next to every remark and corrective action box allows one-tap audio dictation using the browser's speech recognition API.
It transcribes speech into text in real time, keeping field notes detailed and legible.
3. Closed-Loop Maintenance & Regulatory Compliance
🛠️ G. Audit-to-Ticket Corrective Action Loop (CAPA)
The Reality on the Ground: Currently, when a technician checks "DEFECTIVE" or "REPLACEMENT NEEDED" on a Quarterly Audit (e.g., Inverter cooling fan failed, Lightning arrester cable severed, Fire extinguisher expired), that finding sits passively inside a 10-page report.
How It Works:
Admins reviewing the audit can click a single button: "Convert to Work Order" on any failed checklist item.
This creates a dedicated maintenance ticket with:
Defect description + photo from the audit
Assigned technician + target resolution date
Required spare parts (e.g., 1x 50kA SPD module)
The technician sees this on their Staff Dashboard under "Assigned Work Orders" and must upload a "Fixed" verification photo to resolve it.
📋 H. Automated NERC & REA Monthly Compliance Packet Generator
The Reality on the Ground: The Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) and the Rural Electrification Agency (REA) under the Nigeria Electrification Project (NEP) mandate strict monthly KPI reports.
How It Works:
In the Admin view, clicking "Export REA / NERC Monthly Filing" aggregates 30 days of hourly and weekly data for any site.
Automatically calculates mandatory regulatory metrics:
System Availability & Uptime (%)
Renewable Energy Fraction (Solar vs. Diesel %)
Total Energy Delivered to the Community (MWh)
Outage Frequency (SAIFI) & Average Outage Duration (SAIDI)
Exports as an official PDF or Excel sheet ready to submit to regulatory bodies.
🗺️ I. Mini-Grid Topology Health Heatmap (Single-Line Diagram Status)
The Reality on the Ground: Management needs to see the real-time operational status of an entire mini-grid at a glance without reading through 24 hourly rows.
How It Works:
An interactive visual schematic representing the mini-grid power path: $$\text{Solar PV Array} \longrightarrow \text{Inverters / MPPT} \longrightarrow \text{Battery Bank} \longrightarrow \text{Diesel Genset} \longrightarrow \text{T&D Mini-Grid Bus} \longrightarrow \text{Consumers}$$
Each node glows Green (Normal), Amber (Warning/Derated), or Red (Faulted) based on the latest hourly log values (e.g., if inverter temperature is $> 65^\circ\text{C}$ or battery SoC $< 25%$).
